# 🌸 Minecraft Tree Biomes & Canopy Leaves

**`gh-tree`** offers 4 authentic Minecraft tree biomes, each with unique bark textures, leaf hues, and seasonal foliage animations.

---

## 🌲 Biome Varieties (`tree-type`)

| Biome | Tree Type Value | Visual Characteristics | Best Paired With |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Classic Oak 🌳** | `oak` *(default)* | Warm oak bark with cross-hatch grain, deep green canopy foliage, and apple drops. | Sunny or Rain weather |
| **Sakura Blossom 🌸** | `sakura` | Dark cherry bark with soft pastel pink leaves and fluttering pink cherry blossom petals. | Spring / Sunny / Tokyo weather |
| **Taiga Spruce 🌲** | `spruce` | Dark coniferous needles, rough dark spruce bark, and conical canopy shaping. | Snow / Taiga Taiga weather |
| **Birch Forest ⚪** | `birch` | Pale white birch bark with iconic dark notch markings and soft olive green leaves. | Fair weather / Fox companion |

---

## 🍃 Canopy Leaf Commit Intensity Levels (0 to 4)

Your canopy is constructed out of **14 weekly branch leaf blocks**, arranged pyramidally to represent your 14 most recent contribution weeks.

Each block's color richness and density dynamically level up based on your commit volume in that specific week:

```
          [ Tier -3: Peak Block ]
      [ Tier -2: 3 Mid-Upper Blocks ]
    [ Tier -1: 5 Mid Canopy Blocks ]
  [ Tier 0: 5 Lower Wide Canopy Blocks ]
```

### Commit Volume Thresholds:

| Level | Weekly Commits | Oak / Spruce / Birch Foliage | Sakura Blossom Foliage |
| :---: | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **Level 0** | 0 commits | Dormant, dry desaturated olive-gray | Pale pastel gray-pink |
| **Level 1** | 1 – 4 commits | Light spring green | Soft light cherry pink |
| **Level 2** | 5 – 11 commits | Vibrant medium forest green | Rich pastel pink |
| **Level 3** | 12 – 24 commits | Deep lush canopy green | Vivid vibrant sakura blossom |
| **Level 4** | 25+ commits | Ultra-rich emerald canopy green | Radiant royal cherry blossom pink |

> [!TIP]
> Achieving **Level 4** (25+ commits in a single week) also automatically helps unlock the underground **Emerald Ore ❇️**!
