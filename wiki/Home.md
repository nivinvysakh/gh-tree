# Welcome to the gh-tree Wiki! 🌴

**gh-tree** turns your GitHub contributions, commit streaks, and pull requests into living **Minecraft Tree biomes** featuring animated companion pets, leveling milestone chests, underground ores, live weather, and holiday events.

---

Explore our detailed documentation and game mechanics below:

| **[🐾 Collectibles & Game Mechanics](Collectibles-&-Game-Mechanics)** | Pets (Wolf, Fox, Cat), Campfire, Milestone Chests, and Stat Signposts. |
| **[💎 Underground Ores](Underground-Ores)** | All 6 underground ores, maintainer perks, and the **Lapis Lazuli Contributor Badge**! |
